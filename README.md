# 📱 iPhone Sales Analysis on Flipkart (India)

## 📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes iPhone sales data from Flipkart India using Python. The goal is to understand customer preferences, product popularity, pricing trends, and the impact of discounts on customer engagement.

Using **Pandas, NumPy, Matplotlib, and Seaborn**, I performed data cleaning, exploratory data analysis (EDA), and generated business insights from the dataset.

---

## 🎯 Business Questions Solved

1. What are the top 10 highest-rated iPhones on Flipkart?
2. How many ratings do the highest-rated iPhones have?
3. Which iPhone has the highest number of reviews on Flipkart?
4. What is the relationship between sale price and number of ratings?
5. What is the relationship between discount percentage and number of ratings?
6. Which are the least expensive and most expensive iPhones available, along with their specifications?
